SevaOne · Citizen journey

From login → department portal → back to SevaOne

Complete self-service path: the citizen signs in on SevaOne, chooses a service, is sent to the department website (for example Nadakacheri) to finish the form and payment, then returns to the SevaOne dashboard with their application reference.

One sign-in

Citizens authenticate on SevaOne only. The department trusts a one-time pass from SevaOne — they do not create a second login for this handoff.

Where the work happens

Form filling and fee payment happen on the department portal. SevaOne is the front door and the place to track status afterwards.

Coming home

After acknowledgement (or department Log out), the browser returns to SevaOne Dashboard. SevaOne session stays active.
