SevaOne | Architecture and flows
5
Who uses SevaOne
Citizen
Self-service: signs in, discovers services, applies and tracks every application.
Assisted-mode operator
Kiosk staff (Grama One, Karnataka One, Bangalore One style) apply on a citizen's behalf, with their own login and session.
Department staff
Process the application in the department's own system and login, entirely outside SevaOne. Departments integrate once against a published contract.
Government administrator
Five tiers: Superadmin, Chief Minister, Govt Official, District Head, Taluk Head. Read-only analytics, scoped by district or taluk.
SevaOne | Architecture and flows
6
What the platform provides
Identity and sign-in
Mobile OTP for returning citizens, DigiLocker Aadhaar consent for new ones. WSO2 Identity Server issues the session.
Service catalogue
Departments and schemes kept in the discovery schema, with Sakala and SevaSindhu catalogue clients behind it.
Secure department handoff
Single-use encrypted passes carry the citizen to the department and back. Department APIs sit behind WSO2 API Manager.
Application tracking
Orchestration and status schemas record each application. A background poller keeps status in sync.
AI service finder
Ask in English or Kannada. Retrieval over the service index answers or asks a clarifying question.
Government insights
Aggregate-only analytics by district and taluk, plus service notices and notifications for citizens.

SevaOne | Architecture and flows
19
Data architecture
PostgreSQL: one database, six schemas
discovery
departments and service catalogue
portal
citizens, operators, tokens
orchestration
applications, department sessions
status
timelines and payments
audit
who did what
notification
outbound messages
Every read and write goes through a named Postgres function: no inline SQL, no ORM. All keys are uuid. Schema changes ship as Flyway migrations.
Redis
Sessions for citizen, operator, admin and gov-admin, plus the WSO2 token-introspection cache.
Kafka
Events published from the transactional outbox, so a write and its event never drift apart.
AI service store
Its own conversation and index data, with a FAISS vector index. No shared keys with Postgres.
Department data stays in the department's system. SevaOne holds the citizen identity, the catalogue and the application record.
